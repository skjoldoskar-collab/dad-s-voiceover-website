About Me

Josh Skjold is a trained voiceover specialist with over 25 years of experience covering a wide range of services. He has done voice work in radio, educational textbooks, documentary and promotional film, artistic endeavors, commercial production, and full-length feature film.

Beyond the microphone, Josh is an academic teacher, performing singer, songwriter, and instrumentalist. He appeared in the major motion picture 'Thr3e' (as Detective Tom) and is a published creative writer who currently works at the University of Warsaw.
